FAQ

Delivery questions

Do you deliver to Northern Ireland?

Yes. We deliver to all 32 counties on the island of Ireland at the same rates.

Can I collect my order?

Trade customers can arrange collection from our warehouse. Call us to book a time.

How do I track my delivery?

You'll get an email with a tracking link as soon as your order leaves the warehouse.

Can I change or cancel an order?

If it hasn't left the warehouse yet, yes. Ring or email us as soon as possible with your order number.

How do trade pallet deliveries work?

Trade account holders can book scheduled pallet drops. Ask about free delivery on agreed monthly volumes on our contact page.
