Our story

Started in a shed, built on the road

Thirty2 began with one van, a few pallets of oil and a simple idea: Irish motorists and mechanics should be able to get a quality lubricant quickly, whatever county they're in.

We spent years supplying local garages ourselves. The same questions kept coming up. Which grade does this engine need? Can you get it here by tomorrow? Will the price hold for the next order? We built the business around answering them well.

When we chose NK2X as our range, it was because the oils matched how we like to work: clear specs, sensible pack sizes and consistent quality. Today we're still family-run, still answering the phone ourselves, and now delivering across all 32 counties, north and south.
